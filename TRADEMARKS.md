# The IMAGO name and logo

The code in this repository is free software under the licence in [LICENSE](LICENSE). The name
**IMAGO**, its logo and its app icons are not. They identify the official app published by Paulo
Marques, and the licence of the code does not give you the right to use them.

IMAGO is not a registered trademark. This file states how the name and logo may be used; it does
not claim a registration that does not exist.

## What this covers

- The name IMAGO, used as the name of an app or service.
- The logo and symbol, wherever they appear here: the launcher icons in `app/src/main/res/` and
  the wordmark and symbol drawn in
  `core/designsystem/src/commonMain/kotlin/eu/studio742/imago/core/designsystem/ImagoBrand.kt`.

## What you may do

- Build and run the app for yourself, changed or not, with the name and logo, as long as you do
  not distribute that build.
- Refer to IMAGO by name, for example to say that your project is based on it.

## What a fork must change before it is distributed

If you distribute a modified version — on an app store, as a download, or as a hosted service —
give it your own name and your own icon, and change the `applicationId` (`eu.studio742.imago`).
Users must not be led to think your build is the official app or that it is endorsed by its
author.

## Questions

If you are not sure whether a use is allowed, open an issue before you publish.
